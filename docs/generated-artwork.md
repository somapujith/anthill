# Frontier Tech artwork

Created procedurally on November 12, 2026. Final project asset: `public/media/editorial/frontier-tech.jpg` (1600 × 1200, 4:3, JPEG quality 88, about 288 KB). It replaces the stock photograph of an open hand under an infinity loop that illustrated the former "Mitigate Climate Change" sector card, and is conceptual artwork, not a photograph or a diagram of any Anthill portfolio company.

## Method

Authored as a seeded script (seed 2611) rather than prompted. A 3D scene is projected through a fixed perspective camera into an SVG, rendered with Playwright (Chrome) at 3200 × 2400, downsampled to 1600 × 1200, then given a soft vignette, a gentle mid-tone lift and fine monochrome grain with PIL before JPEG export. Everything is deterministic: the same seed reproduces the same file.

## Concept

India's sovereign tech layer (AI and computing, defence, space, clean tech, robotics) as one precise scene. A receding lattice plane carries a raised die slab with a fine sub-grid, a ring of land-grid pads and four corner registration marks. Tilted orbit rings and thin flight arcs, with dotted ground tracks, cross the space above it. A single Anthill-red trajectory is the focal element: it launches from the lattice, climbs in a smooth curve across the front of the slab, is inserted tangentially into one of the orbit rings (a dial with degree ticks) and follows it round to a marker. Dashed red ground track, vertical drop lines and a few red lattice cells give it depth. The lattice dissolves into small registration crosses and every line fades toward the frame edge so the subject survives any crop.

Palette is warm ink charcoal, ivory hairlines (#F3EEE4) and Anthill red #E6212B only, with the soft light sitting at the horizon behind the slab.

## Framing

The whole focal element (red trajectory, ring, slab) sits inside roughly x 380-1220 and y 240-1070 of the 1600 × 1200 canvas, so it holds in the card's 4:3 frame and in 16:9, 1:1 and 4:5 centre crops. Suggested `object-position`: 4:3 `50% 50%`, 16:9 `50% 60%`, 1:1 `50% 50%`, 4:5 `50% 50%`.

## Constraints

No text, letters, numerals, logos or watermarks; no flags or national symbols; no people. No neon glow, glass blobs, floating spheres, sparkles, "AI brain" or circuit-board clip-art. Only the red trajectory carries a faint, tight bloom. Not for use as a depiction of a real facility, product or investment.
