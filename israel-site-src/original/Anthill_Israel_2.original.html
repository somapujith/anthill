Anthill Ventures & II2W

Turning agricultural innovation into commercial growth in India

Anthill Ventures and II2W, Green Road Innovation’s investment initiative, are developing a joint AgriTech investment and company-building platform. The partnership combines Anthill’s decade of investment and Speed Scaling experience with Green Road’s agricultural expertise, access to Israeli innovation and operational presence in Israel and India.

Together, the partners aim to identify promising technologies, validate their performance under local farming conditions and support commercialization through capital, project execution and strategic partnerships.

We invite qualified investors, fund managers and family offices to join us in bringing agricultural innovation to scale in India.

Field validation · Market entry · Scaling

Turning AgriTech investment into commercial growth

Anthill Ventures and Green Road Innovation combine investment with hands-on support to help AgriTech companies succeed in India. We work with companies to adapt their technologies to local crops and farming conditions, validate performance through pilots, assess farmer economics and build commercial partnerships and distribution channels.

The partnership brings together Anthill’s Speed Scaling model and Green Road’s agricultural expertise and project execution capabilities. By testing technical performance and customer demand early, we aim to focus growth capital on companies that demonstrate practical value and a credible path to adoption across the Indian market.

The investment model

Combining capital, field validation and market access

The model combines investment with a structured process for selecting AgriTech companies, validating their solutions under Indian farming conditions and supporting commercial growth.

01

Identification and selection

Identifying technologies that address specific agricultural needs in India and globally, including crop productivity, water efficiency, automation and reduced crop losses.

02

Speed Scaler Studio

Supporting field trials, technology adaptation and commercial validation. Companies assess performance, farmer economics and customer demand while preparing their market entry strategy.

03

Speed Scaler Fund

Investing in selected companies that demonstrate practical value, commercial potential and a credible path to adoption following validation.

04

Commercial growth

Combining Green Road’s agricultural expertise and project execution with Anthill’s market access network to connect companies with growers, agribusinesses, distributors and strategic partners.

Through holding-company structures and Indian joint ventures, the platform aims to capture value from local commercialization and expansion, with potential to serve global markets. These arrangements remain subject to final investment and partnership terms.
