04The investment model

Combining capital, field validation and market access

A structured process for selecting AgriTech companies, validating their solutions under Indian farming conditions and supporting commercial growth.

  1. 01 Select

    Identification and selection

    Technologies that address specific agricultural needs in India and globally.

    • Crop productivity
    • Water efficiency
    • Automation
    • Reduced crop losses
  2. 02 Validate

    Speed Scaler Studio

    Field trials, technology adaptation and commercial validation, ahead of market entry.

    • Performance
    • Farmer economics
    • Customer demand
    • Market entry
  3. 03 Invest

    Speed Scaler Fund

    Investing, following validation, in selected companies that demonstrate:

    • Practical value
    • Commercial potential
    • Credible path to adoption
  4. 04 Scale

    Commercial growth

    Combining Green Road’s agricultural expertise and project execution with Anthill’s market access network to connect companies with:

    • Growers
    • Agribusinesses
    • Distributors
    • Strategic partners

Structure

Through holding-company structures and Indian joint ventures, the platform aims to capture value from local commercialization and expansion, with potential to serve global markets. These arrangements remain subject to final investment and partnership terms.
